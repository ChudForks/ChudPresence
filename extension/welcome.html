1

Enable your Activities.

Required

An Activity detects what you’re watching or listening to on a website. One browser setting gets them running.

IN THE EXTENSIONS PAGE
  1. Open ChudPresence’s extension settings.
  2. Turn on Allow User Scripts.
  3. Return here. We’ll check the setting for you.
Allow User Scripts

Missing the setting in an older browser? Turn on Developer mode on the Extensions page instead. The included YouTube Activity is added once Activities are enabled.

2

Connect your Discord.

Needed to share

Connect Discord to share your detected activity with friends. Your next video or favorite track can become part of your profile.

Want to look around first? Choose Use local preview. To share on Discord, you’ll need to sign in or pair the ChudPresence Link desktop app.

Using ChudPresence Link instead?

Download ChudPresence Link from GitHub, or search for ChudPresence Link in the Microsoft Store. Pair it from the Link tab in the ChudPresence popup. Link can share activity without this Discord sign-in.

3

Try your first Activity.

Next

YouTube is included, so you can try it without downloading anything else. Enable Activities to get it ready.

YouTubeYour first Activity · Included
  1. Play a YouTube video.
  2. Open ChudPresence from your browser’s Extensions menu (the puzzle piece).
  3. Check your Local presence preview. If it stays blank, reload the video’s tab.
Ready when you are.

Enable Activities to finish. You can connect Discord later.
