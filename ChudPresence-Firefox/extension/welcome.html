GET STARTED

Your browser activity,
on your Discord profile.

First, let’s get your first Activity running. You choose which websites to add and when to connect Discord.

Checking your setup…

  1. 1

    Enable Activities

    Required

    An Activity teaches ChudPresence how to detect what you’re watching or listening to on a website.

    Click below to request Firefox’s User Scripts permission. In the Firefox prompt, review the warning, select “Allow unverified third-party scripts to access your data”, then choose Allow. This lets the Activities you install run on websites you approve.

    Your Services list is empty until this is enabled. The included YouTube Activity will be added automatically.

  2. 2

    Try your first Activity

    Next

    YouTube is included, so you can try it without downloading anything else. Enable Activities above to get it ready.

    Play a YouTube video, then click ChudPresence in Firefox’s Extensions menu (the puzzle piece) to see your Local presence preview. Existing tabs are detected automatically; reload the video’s tab if it stays blank.

    Browse more Activities

    In the Library, choose Refresh catalog and approve catalog access. Install an Activity for each site you use, reviewing its requested website access.

  3. 3

    Connect Discord

    Optional

    Sign in to Discord to share detected activity on your profile. You can try the local preview first and connect later from the ChudPresence popup.

Ready when you are.

Enable Activities to finish. Discord can wait.

Set up later

You can reopen this guide from the popup’s Settings or the Activity Library.
